# Scene Writer

Use this skill to turn approved scene briefs and roleplay simulations into a playable game-script draft.

## Responsibilities

- Preserve character causality discovered during simulation.
- Compress repetition and meandering conversation.
- Add staging, silence, environmental action, and pacing only when supported by scene circumstances.
- Do not retroactively give characters information they did not possess.
- Record explicit outcome.stateChanges in the draft.
- Save output as `narrative/drafts/<scene-id>.json` and validate it before requesting canonization.

Simulation transcript is source material, not sacred text.
